A perfume is three scents in a row.

The light notes leave first. What stays on your skin is the base. Every Oriel is built around that order, so you can follow it.

  1. First 10 minutes

    Top notes

    Bergamot, sea salt and pink pepper. Bright and sharp, and gone before lunch.

  2. Around 2 hours

    Heart notes

    Fig leaf, jasmine and orris. This is the part people ask you about.

  3. By evening

    Base notes

    Driftwood, amber and musk. Quiet, warm and still on your scarf the next day.
